## 📌 Overview

**Career Copilot** is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to help students and job seekers understand their current skills, identify gaps against target roles, and create an actionable, personalized path toward career readiness.

Instead of using disparate tools for:
- Resume parsing & scoring
- Job matching
- Skill-gap breakdown
- Career roadmapping
- Interview preparation
- Career guidance & mentoring

Career Copilot consolidates these workflows into an end-to-end intelligent platform powered by local LLM inference.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students and job seekers face significant friction when preparing for industry roles:
- **Uncertainty:** Inability to gauge if a resume passes industry thresholds.
- **Ambiguity:** Job descriptions list laundry lists of requirements that are difficult to prioritize.
- **Disconnection:** Interview practice and coursework often have no tie-in to actual target role gaps.
- **Generic Advice:** Standard career portals supply generic tips without evaluating actual baseline competencies.

> **"What exactly should I do next to become job-ready?"**  
> Career Copilot is engineered to answer that question systematically.

---

## 💡 Solution

Career Copilot connects resume intelligence, role requirements, skill benchmarking, actionable roadmaps, and mock interviews into a closed feedback loop:
